[h1]Syx Auto-Planner - Intelligent Room Builder & Furnisher[/h1]

[b]Syx Auto-Planner[/b] is an intelligent room planning and layout assistant for [i]Songs of Syx[/i]. It integrates directly with the native Room Editor toolbar to eliminate ceiling collapses and optimize room furnishing in a single click.

[h2]⚡ Highlights & Features[/h2]

[b]1. Auto-Strut (1-Click Ceiling Support Solver):[/b]
[list]
[*] Calculates full 16-ray structural support using the game's exact ceiling physics ([i]TileRayTracer[/i]).
[*] Scores candidate pillar locations by marginal support gain.
[*] Multi-pass engine synchronization verifies that [b]0 yellow tiles remain[/b] after placement.
[*] Respects door clearance (leaves door threshold open) and preserves room floor connectivity via topological simple-point verification.
[/list]

[b]2. Auto-Furnish (Interleaved Density & Efficiency Packing):[/b]
[list]
[*] Automatically places workstations, dormitory beds, or storage crates using effective output and spatial density heuristics ([i]multiplierStats[/i] weighted by area).
[*] Interleaves auxiliary efficiency modules along walls and pillars to strictly hit the configured target room efficiency (100%).
[*] Features automatic workstation backtracking if initial placement leaves insufficient room for required auxiliary modules.
[*] 5x5 / 7x7 stockpile optimization packs maximum crate volume with minimal wasted corridors.
[/list]

[b]3. Zero-GC Reachability Flood-Fill:[/b]
[list]
[*] Array-based BFS flood-fill verifies that all workstation interaction tiles are reachable from room doorways.
[*] Includes deterministic virtual doorway fallback if you plan rooms before cutting doorways into walls.
[*] Enforces [i]WillBlockRoom[/i] floor integrity so no isolated 1x1 floor pockets are trapped behind items.
[/list]

[b]4. Quick-Build (1-Click Combo):[/b]
[list]
[*] Executes Auto-Strut followed immediately by Auto-Furnish with a single click.
[/list]

[h2]🎮 In-Game Controls & Hotkey[/h2]
[list]
[*] Drag your room outline using the native room placement tool.
[*] The compact Auto-Planner box docks directly under the Room Editor panel.
[*] Press [b][F9][/b] or click [b]'AUTO'[/b] in the top HUD bar to open settings (packing strategy, target efficiency, internal storage ratio, language).
[*] Fully bilingual in [b]English[/b] and [b]Deutsch[/b].
[/list]
