[h1]Syx Architect - Creative, Schönbau & Sandbox Suite[/h1]

[b]Syx Architect[/b] transforms [i]Songs of Syx[/i] into the ultimate model-building sandbox! Whether you want to construct colossal Roman-style metropolises, design sprawling paved road systems, enjoy the charming atmosphere of bustling workers without tedious resource grinding, or customize weather and population on the fly, Syx Architect puts complete control at your fingertips.

[h2]⚡ Highlights & Features[/h2]

[b]1. Semantic Schönbau Tiers (Decoupling Work from Material Costs):[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Baustellen-Modus (Construction):[/b]
  - [i]Free Materials (Keep Work):[/i] Your oddjobbers physically pave roads, build walls, and erect grand halls—the bustling street life remains 100% active, but no materials are drained from your stockpiles!
  - [i]Fast & Free (10x Speed):[/i] Supercharged building pace at zero resource cost.
  - [i]Instant Build:[/i] Completes all pending rooms, roads, walls, and fortifications across the entire map immediately.
[*] [b]Gebäude-Wartung (Maintenance):[/b]
  - [i]Janitors Work (Free Mats):[/i] Janitors roam the city repairing buildings, keeping the bustling atmosphere alive with zero repair resource consumption.
  - [i]Zero Decay:[/i] Permanently locks all building condition at 100%.
[*] [b]Wissen & Forschung (Knowledge):[/b]
  - [i]No Forgetting (High-Watermark):[/i] Unlocked technologies are never forgotten, even during lab worker shortages.
  - [i]Omniscience:[/i] Grants +1,000,000 innovation points instantly.
[*] [b]Lagerhaus-Logistik (Logistics):[/b]
  - Automatically maintains building materials or all assigned warehouse crates at ~80% capacity.
[/list]
