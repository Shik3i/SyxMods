Welcome to Syx Architect!
Your ultimate creative, model-building, and sandbox suite for Songs of Syx (v0.71+).

OPENING IN GAME:
- Press [F10] on your keyboard anytime.
- Click the permanent 'Syx Architect [F10]' button in the top HUD bar.
- Or use the developer menu entry [Syx Architect].

CORE CAPABILITIES:

1. SEMANTIC CHEAT TIERS (DECOUPLING WORK FROM COSTS):
   - Construction Tiers:
     * Free Materials (Keep Work): Your builders construct and pave normally,
       preserving living city ambience, without consuming any stockpile goods.
     * Fast & Free (10x Speed): 10x accelerated construction at zero cost.
     * Instant Build: Instantly completes all designated rooms, roads, plazas,
       walls, roofs, and fortifications.
   - Building Maintenance:
     * Janitors Work (Free Mats): Janitors roam and repair without materials.
     * Zero Decay: Permanently freezes structural integrity at 100%.
   - Knowledge & Research:
     * No Forgetting (High-Watermark): Unlocked tech stays unlocked forever.
     * Omniscience: Grants +1,000,000 innovation points immediately.
   - Warehouse Logistics:
     * Raw Materials Only: Keeps base construction goods filled to ~80%.
     * All Assigned Crates: Keeps all designated crates topped up to ~80%.

2. WORLD, NATURE & POPULATION MANAGER:
   - Instant Immigration: Spawn +10, +50, or +100 citizens of any selected
     species to populate new quarters immediately.
   - Cosmopolitan Wave: Spawn +25 citizens across all playable races at once!
   - Anti-Aging Protection: Vastly extends longevity so no citizens die of old age.
   - Clear Daylight & Weather: Locks peaceful daylight and dispels clouds, rain,
     and snow - ideal for architecture and screenshots.
   - 100% Soil Fertility: Maximizes map moisture for lush farms and orchards.

3. SECURITY & PEACE:
   - Raid Immunity: Automatically cancels hostile raids and barbarian incursions.
   - Combat Victory: Breaks enemy morale immediately for an unconditional win.

4. 1-CLICK INSTANT ACTIONS:
   - [[>>] Instant Build Now] for the entire settlement.
   - [[++] Fill All Crates to 100%] across all warehouses.
   - [[$$] +1,000,000 Denari] to replenish the royal treasury.
   - [[XX] Win Combat Now] on all tactical combat fields.

Happy building and shaping your dream metropolis!
