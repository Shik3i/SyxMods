Welcome to Syx Architect!
The comprehensive creative, Schönbau, and sandbox suite for Songs of Syx (v0.71+).

OPENING THE SUITE:
- Press [F10] on your keyboard anywhere in-game.
- Click the discreet 'Syx Architect [F10]' button in the top HUD bar.
- Open via the debug menu [Syx Architect].

CORE CAPABILITIES:

1. SEMANTIC SCHÖNBAU TIERS:
   - Construction:
     * Free Materials (Keep Work): Oddjobbers physically pave roads and build
       walls, but material costs are 0! Enjoy the bustling city life.
     * Fast & Free (10x Speed): Supercharged building speed at zero cost.
     * Instant Build: Immediately completes all planned rooms, roads, walls,
       ceilings, and fortifications with one click.
   - Maintenance:
     * Janitors Work (Free Mats): Janitors roam the city repairing buildings,
       keeping the atmosphere alive without draining resources.
     * Zero Decay: Permanently locks building condition at 100%.
   - Knowledge:
     * No Forgetting (High-Watermark): Unlocked technologies are never lost!
     * Omniscience: Grants +1,000,000 innovation points instantly.
   - Logistics:
     * Raw Materials: Auto-tops up essential building resources to ~80%.
     * All Assigned Crates: Supplies all assigned warehouse crates automatically.

2. WORLD, NATURE & POPULATION MANAGER:
   - Instant Immigrants: Spawn +10, +50, or +100 citizens of any playable species
     directly to populate your grand districts.
   - Cosmopolitan Wave: Spawn +25 citizens of every playable species at once!
   - Anti-Aging: Multiplies life expectancy by 1000x to prevent deaths from old age.
   - Clear Building Weather: Freezes bright daylight, clears storm clouds, rain,
     and snow for crystal-clear model building and screenshot captures.
   - 100% Soil Fertility: Maximizes soil moisture map-wide, letting you place farms
     and lush orchards anywhere without ground restrictions.

3. SECURITY & PEACE:
   - Raid Immunity: Automatically cancels barbarian raids and hostile incursions.
   - Combat Auto-Win: Instantly shatters enemy morale for guaranteed victory.

4. 1-CLICK INSTANT ACTIONS:
   - [⚡ Instant Build Now] settlement-wide.
   - [📦 Fill All Crates to 100%] in all warehouses.
   - [⚔️ Win Combat Now] on all active battlefields.

Enjoy designing your dream empire!
