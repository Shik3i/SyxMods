                           SYX ARCHITECT - CREATIVE SUITE

Welcome to Syx Architect!
This suite gives you full creative and sandbox control over your Songs of Syx city.

KEY FEATURES:
1. Boosts & Innovation:
   Instantly boost technology progression, research speed, and city innovation
   points with customizable multiplier tiers (2x, 5x, 10x, 100x).

2. Zero Degradation & Spoilage:
   Keep every structure in pristine condition forever without maintenance costs.
   Food, rations, and perishable resources will never spoil in your granaries.

3. Instant Build & Creative Schönbau:
   Layout ambitious monuments, intricate road networks, and palatial districts
   without waiting for haulers, brickmakers, or scaffolding builders.

4. Logistics & Warehousing:
   Multiply crate storage sizes to eliminate micro-management bottlenecks.

5. Sandbox Peace & Raid Immunity:
   Focus entirely on architecture and city aesthetics without worrying about
   unexpected devastation from barbarian raids.

HOW TO OPEN IN-GAME:
- Press the configured hotkey or open the debug panel in the top-right menu.
- Select 'Syx Architect' to toggle this control panel at any time.

Enjoy building your dream metropolis!
